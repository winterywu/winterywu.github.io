# Privacy Policy

This Google Cloud OAuth client is used only by its owner (winterywu) to run
[rclone](https://rclone.org/) for personal backups to the owner's own Google Drive.

- It is not offered to, and is not used by, anyone else.
- No data is collected, stored, shared, or sold by this application.
- Files are transferred directly between the owner's computer and the owner's
  Google Drive; nothing passes through any third-party server.
- Access can be revoked at any time at
  <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.
